Every feature in Yotara serves one purpose: helping you focus on what matters.

Full control over your tasks

Three views — Inbox, Today, and Upcoming — give you full visibility into what needs your attention. No configuration required.

  • checkInbox view — your capture hub. Everything lands here first.
  • checkToday view — what's urgent and due right now.
  • checkUpcoming — plan your week and see what's ahead.
  • checkPriority levels (low, medium, high) with color indicators.
  • checkDue dates and completion tracking with calendar picker.

Projects, labels, and buckets

Organize work your way. Color-coded projects group related tasks. Labels add cross-cutting tags. Buckets help you enter deep focus mode.

  • checkProjects with customizable colors — work, personal, any category.
  • checkLabels for cross-cutting tags like "urgent" or "design review."
  • checkBucket system for deep focus sessions — batch related work.
  • checkFilter across any view by project, label, or priority.

Set it and forget it

Daily standups, weekly reports, monthly bills. Recurring tasks materialize automatically on completion — you'll never miss a repeating task again.

  • checkDaily, weekdays, weekly, monthly, and yearly intervals.
  • checkTasks materialize on completion — not on a fixed schedule.
  • checkWorks with subtasks and priority levels.
  • checkVisual indicator shows the recurrence pattern.

Break it down

Split big tasks into manageable subtasks. Track progress at a glance with the completion bar. One level deep keeps things simple — no infinite nesting.

  • checkOne level deep — enough to break things down, not enough to get lost.
  • checkProgress bar on the parent task updates automatically.
  • checkParent project inheritance — subtasks belong to the same project.
  • checkWorks with recurring tasks for repeating checklists.

Write with markdown

Format task notes with markdown and preview them live. The built-in toolbar means you don't need to remember syntax. Output is sanitized and safe.

  • checkFormat toolbar for bold, italic, headings, lists, and links.
  • checkLive preview — see the rendered result as you type.
  • checkDOMPurify sanitized HTML output — safe for display.
  • checkCode blocks with monospace formatting.

Your data, your rules

Export everything as JSON or CSV with one click. Self-hosted means your data never leaves your server. No vendor lock-in. No surprises.

  • checkExport all tasks to JSON — full fidelity, machine-readable.
  • checkExport to CSV — open in any spreadsheet application.
  • checkDirect SQLite database access — query your data directly.
  • checkNo vendor lock-in — your data is always portable.
